03Find your scent

We will find a scent for your request

We will suggest several options: a decant, a miniature or a full-size bottle, depending on what suits you best. The consultation is free.

Know which scent you want?

Send us its name or describe it in your own words. Tell us the volume you need or the budget you would like to stay within, and we will suggest options.

Not sure what to choose?

Answer the 5 survey questions and tell us your budget. Send your answers wherever is easiest: email, Instagram, Telegram, VK or MAX. We will choose several options for you.

This website does not store or collect anything. You send your answers to us yourself, in an ordinary conversation.

  1. Where do you feel good?

    You can choose several.

    • a coffee shop with pastries and cinnamon
    • a forest after the rain
    • the sea, salt, a white shirt
    • an evening bar, velvet, candles
    • a garden in bloom in spring

  2. Which fragrance have you loved, or love now?

    A name, or simply a scent you remember on someone.

  3. Which scents are definitely not for you?

    For example: sweet, powdery, tobacco, old-fashioned florals, sharp freshness.

  4. Where will you wear it?

    • every day and to work
    • on dates and in the evening
    • I want one signature scent for everything
    • it is a gift: who for, and what are they like?

  5. What would you like people to think when you walk past?

    • “what a clean, fresh scent”
    • “I want to know who that is”
    • “it feels calm and cosy around them”
    • “this person knows their worth”

  6. Your budget

    Roughly how much you would like to spend: on decants, a box or a full-size bottle.

04For brands and distributors

We introduce people to fragrances

A customer wears a decant of 2 ml or more for several days (2 ml is about 40 sprays), gets to know the scent on their own skin, and buys the one they love as a full bottle. That is how we work, and that is the role we would like to play for your brand in Russia.

  • Full-size bottles for every customer. We sell full bottles to anyone who wants them, including customers who first met a scent in one of our boxes.

How it works

Four steps to your scent

Write to us

Wherever is easiest: Instagram, VK, Telegram, MAX or email (all contacts). Tell us what you chose or who the gift is for.

Free consultation

We ask a couple of questions about taste and mood and suggest options.

Gift wrapping

A box, a ribbon, tissue paper and bubble wrap with little hearts. Ready to give.

Delivery

In person by arrangement in Saint Petersburg. Across Russia by CDEK, Yandex Delivery, 5Post and Russian Post, as well as via Ozon and Wildberries.

Originals only

We work only with original fragrances in every format, from full-size bottles to the decants in our advent calendars and boxes.

Simple words

We talk about fragrances in an easy, clear way: the mood of a scent, how it unfolds on the skin and what to wear it with.
