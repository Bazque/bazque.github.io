For brands and distributors

We introduce people to fragrances

A customer wears a 2 ml decant for several days, gets to know the scent on their own skin, and buys the one they love as a full bottle. That is how we work, and that is the role we would like to play for your brand in Russia.

5sales channels: Instagram, VK, Telegram, MAX, Avito
  • Discovery that leads to full bottles. We build discovery sets around a brand’s line and “for him and for her” pairs, then sell full bottles to the customers who fell in love with a scent.
  • Your story, told in Russian. Every scent comes with a card: its mood, what to layer it with, how to apply it. We explain fragrances through images and feelings, without technical jargon.
  • Content that features your brand. We film decanting, packing and unboxing for social media and always tag the brand.
  • Gift-ready packaging. Cream boxes with a chocolate ribbon, tissue paper and bubble wrap with little hearts. Our sets are made to be given as presents.
  • Originals only. We decant only from original bottles, and we will gladly show where our stock comes from.

We are open to discussing wholesale terms, permission to decant your fragrances into our discovery boxes, testers for content and joint promotion.

How it works

Four steps to your scent

Write to us

Wherever is easiest: Instagram, VK, Telegram, MAX or email (all contacts). Tell us what you chose or who the gift is for.

Free consultation

We ask a couple of questions about taste and mood and suggest options.

Gift wrapping

A box, a ribbon, tissue paper and bubble wrap with little hearts. Ready to give.

Delivery

In person by arrangement in Saint Petersburg. Across Russia by CDEK, Yandex Delivery, 5Post and Russian Post, as well as via Ozon and Wildberries.

Originals only

We decant only from original bottles. Our samples and miniatures are original too.

Simple words

We describe fragrances through images and moods, without technical jargon.

Get in touch

For partnership enquiries, email is the best way to reach us. We reply in English and Russian.
